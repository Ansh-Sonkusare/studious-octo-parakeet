## Key inferences for the MVP

- Scale of the problem: 1.3 billion adults remain outside formal accounts (2024), and the 2024 survey shows account ownership of 79% globally. The problem is less about access and more about use: ~300 million accounts are idle.
- Fraud losses are rising fastest in the US and UK, where reported losses are concentrated in investment and impersonation scams. In India, RBI bank-fraud value is up while case count is down, so the two sources need separate treatment.
- Credit-stressed households and small businesses: US card balances are back near record levels (USD 1.26 trillion), India's household debt is 45.5% of GDP and rising, and India's delayed MSME payments run at Rs 55,244 crore across 256,892 applications.
- Protection and savings gaps are large but dated in several cases (WEF 2017 retirement gap, S&P 2014 literacy). Use 2024 to 2026 figures where possible.

## Conflicts

- **India DEA Fund balances**: Rs 72,454 crore (Jan 28, 2026) vs Rs 86,917 crore (Jun 30, 2026) vs Rs 98,073 crore (Jan 31, 2026, one report). Verify against Rajya Sabha answers.
- **India cyber fraud 2025**: Rs 19,813 crore (I4C data, ~21.77 lakh complaints) vs Rs 22,495 crore (Parliament reply). Not additive with RBI bank-fraud figures, which cover different frauds and reporting rules.
- **India household debt**: 45.5% of GDP (March 2026, RBI FSR June 2026 per LoansJagat) vs 45.8% (Rs 158.5 lakh crore liabilities, RBI Bulletin Aug 2026 per another report). Likely different publications or dates.
- **Swiss Re natural catastrophe insured share, 2024**: Swiss Re's own summary says 57% uninsured; a UNDRR-hosted copy says 57% insured. The USD 181 billion gap is consistent with the uninsured reading.
- **US total household debt Q2 2026**: USD 18.771 trillion vs 18.794 trillion across outlets. Use the NY Fed figure.
- **Remittance global average**: 6.36% (Sept 2025 report, current homepage) vs 6.49% (Mar 2025 report, older cache).
- **RBI card and digital fraud, FY25 vs FY26**: 13,332 cases (Rs 517 crore) fell to 293 cases (Rs 29 crore). The drop is far steeper than other fraud categories; verify before using.
