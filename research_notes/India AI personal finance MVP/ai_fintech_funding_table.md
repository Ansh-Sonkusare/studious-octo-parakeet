## Key Question 1: Which AI fintech startups raised money in 2025-2026, and how much?

### Takeaway
In the verified rounds, the largest AI-native fintech raises were Nirvana ($100M Series D, Dec 2025, insurance), Rogo ($160M Series D, Apr 2026), Basis ($100M Series B, Feb 2026), Sardine ($70M Series C, Feb 2025, compliance), and Rillet ($70M Series B, Aug 2025, accounting). Accounting, insurance, and compliance agents took the largest verified dollars. Consumer wealth and trading/quant rounds were small or thin, except for Moment ($36M, Jul 2025). Verified 2025 USD rounds total about $0.51B across 13 rows. Verified 2026 rounds total about $1.0B across 14 rows, though 2026 covers only nine months.

### Inferences
- Verified 2026 dollars concentrate in accounting, tax, and audit agents sold to firms (Basis, Accrual, Modus). Most of these companies sell to accounting firms, not directly to businesses. Integral is the exception among the rows above.
- In 2025, the largest verified rounds were insurance (Nirvana, $100M), compliance (Sardine, $70M), and accounting ERP (Rillet, $70M; Campfire, $65M). Agent payments (Catena, Crossmint) and wealth/investment tools (Range, Moment) follow.
- Crunchbase (per search summary, not fetched) reports fintech funding up ~23% YoY in H1 2026, with deal count down >25%. This suggests fewer, larger checks. The source URL was not confirmed, so treat this as unverified.
- Personal finance for consumers remains thin in verified data. The India personal-finance rows are Finanjo (2026, pre-seed, ~$180K) and Bachatt (date UNCLEAR). Quartz (UK, date UNCLEAR) is the only other consumer personal-finance row. OnFinance AI and ArthAlpha are B2B or institutional.
- The 2026 total covers about nine months and the 2025 total covers twelve, so the two years are not directly comparable.

### Gaps
- Inc42 "Funding Galore" and Entrackr 2025 roundups were searched but not located. The India 2025 AI personal-finance coverage is thin: only ArthAlpha, OnFinance AI, and Bachatt (date UNCLEAR) surfaced.
- Consumer wealth and trading/quant rounds beyond those listed were not searched in depth. Trading/quant 2025 rows are Moment, ArthAlpha, and Tradoor (crypto), which are unequal in scope.
- The 2025 coverage is sampled from about 8 searches, not from a full database. Major 2025 AI fintech rounds could be missing (for example, many US personal finance and AI tax startups).
- Tax-specific companies beyond Accrual were not searched.
- Several dollar figures rely on aggregators (Seedtable, Dealroom, Caplight, LeadMagic). These need checking against primary press releases before use.
- Fintech Business Weekly could not be located in search results. Used Qubit Capital, Fintech Futures, and Fintech.global weekly and monthly roundups instead.
