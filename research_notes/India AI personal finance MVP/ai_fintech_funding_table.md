Status as of 2026-10-07. Coverage is partial: the search budget covered 2026 rounds only. No 2025 rounds were researched, and YC batches other than W26 were not researched. The table has 28 rows (14 funded, 14 YC W26), below the 40 to 80 target. Every row has a source URL. Items marked UNCLEAR have conflicting or missing details.

## Key Question 1: Which AI fintech startups raised money in 2025-2026, and how much?

### Takeaway
In the 2026 rounds that could be verified, the largest disclosed AI-native fintech raises were Rogo ($160M Series D, Apr 2026), Basis ($100M Series B, Feb 2026), Modus ($85M seed + Series A, Apr 2026), and Accrual ($75M, Feb 2026). Accounting, tax, and compliance agents took most of the verified dollars. Consumer wealth and trading/quant produced no verified 2026 rounds in this search. 2025 coverage is a gap.

### Inferences
- Verified 2026 dollars concentrate in accounting, tax, and audit agents sold to firms (Basis, Accrual, Modus). Most of these companies sell to accounting firms, not directly to businesses. Integral is the exception among the rows above.
- Crunchbase (per search summary, not fetched) reports fintech funding up ~23% YoY in H1 2026, with deal count down >25%. This suggests fewer, larger checks. The source URL was not confirmed, so treat this as unverified.
- Among the rows above, personal finance is thin: only Finanjo (India, pre-seed, ~$180K) is verified. This is a gap for the India personal-finance MVP.

### Gaps
- 2025 rounds (Jan to Dec 2025) were not researched. The "Jan 2025 to Oct 2026" scope is only half covered (2026 only).
- Consumer wealth (AI robo-advisors, budgeting apps) and trading/quant: no verified 2026 AI-native rounds found.
- Tax-specific companies beyond Accrual, and most insurtech and compliance names beyond the rows above, were not searched.
- YC batches S25, F25, X25, W25, and S26 were not searched. YC rows above are W26 only, and batch membership is taken from secondary listings.
- Target of 40 to 80 rows not met: 28 rows in the table, 14 of which have no funding data.
- Fintech Business Weekly could not be located in search results. Used Qubit Capital, Fintech Futures, and Fintech.global weekly and monthly roundups instead.
- Several dollar figures rely on aggregators (Seedtable, Dealroom, Caplight, LeadMagic). These need checking against primary press releases before use.

## Key Question 2: Which YC fintech companies (2025-2026 batches) are building AI agents for finance?

### Takeaway
Only W26 was searched. From the W26 breakdown, the clearest finance-agent companies are Fenrock AI (AI agents for banking back-office and financial crime), FullSeam (AI employee for finance and accounting teams), End Close (AI reconciliation for payments companies), and Balance (AI accounting). Agent-era payments companies (Maven, Sponge, Orthogonal) also appear in W26.

### Cited Findings
- Fenrock AI (W26): AI agents for banking back-office and financial crime compliance work. [Extruct](https://www.extruct.ai/research/ycw26); [VC Backed](https://vcbacked.co/yc/industry/fintech)

- End Close (W26): AI-powered reconciliation for high-volume payments companies. [VC Backed](https://vcbacked.co/yc/industry/fintech)
- Maven, Sponge, Orthogonal (W26): payments and financial infrastructure for voice and AI agents. [Extruct](https://www.extruct.ai/research/ycw26)
- Extruct counts 37 AI-agent companies in W26 (~19% of 199). [Extruct](https://www.extruct.ai/research/ycw26)

### Inferences
- The share of agent companies in W26 is lower than S25 (~49%, per a Substack profile not fetched). Agent infrastructure for payments is the main fintech sub-cluster.

### Gaps
- No S25, F25, X25, W25, or S26 batch fintech lists were checked.
- YC directory filter "Fintech" (ycombinator.com/companies) was not accessed. Batch membership relies on secondary listings.
- Diligent AI is YC-backed per a search summary, but its batch was not verified.

## Key Question 3: Group counts by category to show where capital is flowing

### Takeaway
Of the 14 verified 2026 funded rows, accounting/tax/audit has the most (5), followed by compliance/KYC (2), insurance (2), payments/agentic commerce (2), and data/research (1), plus advisor tools (1) and personal finance (1). Consumer wealth and trading/quant have none verified. Count is partial and not a measure of total market activity.

### Cited Findings
Counts from the fact table (rows 1 to 14 only; YC rows excluded because they have no funding data):
- SMB finance/accounting: 4 (Basis, Modus, Integral, Neno). Tax: 1 (Accrual). Subtotal accounting/tax: 5.
- Compliance/KYC: 2 (Diligent AI, Spektr).
- Insurance: 2 (Harper, Fulcrum).
- Payments/agentic commerce: 2 (Natural, Airwallex).
- Data/research: 1 (Rogo).
- Advisor tools: 1 (Uptiq, category UNCLEAR).
- Personal finance app: 1 (Finanjo).
- Consumer wealth: 0. Trading/quant: 0.

Dollar totals of latest rounds, rough and mixing currencies: about $1.0B across the 14 rows (Rogo $160M, Basis $100M, Modus $85M, Accrual $75M, Airwallex $320M, Harper $46.8M, Natural $30M, Fulcrum $25M, Uptiq $25M, Spektr ~$20M, Integral EUR 18M, Neno EUR 6.6M, Diligent ~$2.5M, Finanjo ~$0.18M). Airwallex is the largest single round but is only AI-positioned. Excluding Airwallex, the total is roughly $0.7B.

### Inferences
- Capital in this sample is going to agents that do professional work (audit, tax, bookkeeping, banking back office), sold to firms, not to consumers.
- Consumer AI personal finance is underrepresented in verified data. The India personal-finance category shows only one pre-seed round.

### Gaps
- Counts are based only on 2026 search results and a limited sample. They do not represent full market totals.
- Category assignments for Uptiq, Airwallex, and Modus are judgment calls.
- No 2025 data, so no year-over-year comparison is possible.
