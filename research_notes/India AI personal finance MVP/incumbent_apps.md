# Incumbent Indian Retail Investing / Wealth Apps: Teardown (as of Oct 2026)

Scope: Groww, Zerodha (Kite/Coin/Varsity), Upstox, Angel One, Paytm Money, INDmoney, Kuvera, ET Money, Dhan, Scripbox, Fisdom, Jar, smallcase. Indian fiscal year (FY) runs April to March: FY25 = Apr 2024 to Mar 2025, FY26 = Apr 2025 to Mar 2026. "Cr" = crore (10 million INR). Research was done in Oct 2026 under a limited tool budget. Most figures come from secondary press coverage of filings (Entrackr, Inc42, Medianama, Business Standard), not from the filings themselves. Each figure is dated. Older data is flagged.

## 1. Core products per app and which product drives revenue

### Takeaway
Every large incumbent makes most of its money from trading, especially F&O (futures and options) brokerage. Mutual funds are the product that brings users in, but they earn almost nothing on direct plans. At Groww, equity derivatives were still 55% of operating revenue in Q4 FY26. At Angel One, F&O brokerage was 47% of gross income in Q4 FY26. Dhan earns about 88% of its revenue from brokerage. MF-first apps (Kuvera, Scripbox, INDmoney, Paytm Money, ET Money, smallcase) are small or loss-making by comparison.

### Inferences
- The industry pattern is to use MFs and SIPs as a low-margin, high-trust way to bring users in, then earn from F&O, cash trading, MTF and lending. An MF-only AI-native entrant would be competing in the segment where incumbents earn the least. That is easier to enter but needs a different way to make money (advice fees, subscriptions, or regular-plan commissions if it registers as a distributor).
- MTF and lending are now the fastest-growing revenue lines at Groww and Zerodha. This shows incumbents shifting from earning on activity to earning on balances.

### Gaps
- No precise MF revenue figure (commission or AMC fees from the EOP framework) was found for any platform. Groww does not break out MF revenue.
- Upstox, Paytm Money and INDmoney product-level revenue splits were not found.
- From background knowledge, not verified this session: INDmoney offers US stocks and was historically known for them; Paytm Money and Upstox offer MFs, stocks and F&O; Scripbox and Fisdom have historically earned from regular-plan or advisory models. These should be checked.

## 3. Licenses held

### Takeaway
The large brokers hold SEBI stock broker and depository participant registrations and use MF distribution or EOP status for MFs. Groww now also has an NBFC, an AMC, and wealth/PMS (via Fisdom). Any app that wants to give personalised MF advice needs SEBI RIA registration; brokers and EOPs that add AI "recommendations" are in a regulatory grey zone.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI set up the Execution-Only Platform (EOP) framework for direct MF plans in June 2023. Category 1 EOPs register with AMFI and need ₹1 Cr net worth. Category 2 EOPs (stock brokers) register through exchanges and keep about ₹10 lakh deposited. EOPs cannot offer regular plans and may charge AMCs a fixed fee. Platforms run by registered advisers or brokers that serve only their own advisory or broking clients are exempt. — [Business Standard Jun 2023](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/mutual-fund/sebi-directs-mf-platforms-to-become-execution-only-to-cut-out-distributor-123061300956_1.html); [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/sebi-mandates-registration-for-execution-only-platform-providers-offering-mutual-fund-schemes-385523-2023-06-14); [Business Standard Oct 2023](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-asks-brokers-functioning-in-eop-to-maintain-minimum-capital-deposit-123100601024_1.html); [Business Standard AMFI norms](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/amfi-sets-rs-1-crore-net-worth-criteria-for-eops-releases-guidelines-123090100961_1.html)
- Groww entities: Groww CreditServ (NBFC), Growwmf (AMC), Fisdom (wealth, PMS/AIF distribution through Groww W). Groww surrendered its RBI payment aggregator licence in April 2026. — [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-groww-q4-fy26-fo-user-share-decline-customer-shift-mutual-funds-etfs/)
- SEBI treats portfolio analysis (which AI can do) differently from personalised investment advice (which needs RIA registration). Execution-first apps have added AI fund recommendations without an advisory licence. These sources are vendor blogs and need checking against SEBI circulars. — [Genvest](https://www.genvest.ai/blog/ai-wealth-advisor-india-guide); [Invsify](https://invsify.com/blog/best-ai-investment-advisor)

### Inferences
- An AI-native MF MVP has three licensing routes:
  - **AMFI EOP Category 1 (₹1 Cr net worth):** direct plans only, AMC fees, no advice.
  - **SEBI RIA:** can advise and charge the client a fee, but cannot take commissions.
  - **AMFI ARN distributor:** regular plans and trail commission, with "incidental" advice only.
- The choice of route decides whether an AI chat can legally give personalised recommendations.

### Gaps
- No entity-by-entity licence registry was compiled this session (for example, which of Zerodha, Upstox, INDmoney or Paytm Money hold RIA or EOP registration, insurance broking licences, or Account Aggregator FIU status). Check the SEBI intermediary search and the AMFI EOP list.
- From background knowledge, not verified this session: INDmoney has historically operated a SEBI RIA entity (INDwealth/Finzoom), and Zerodha set up an AMC JV with smallcase in 2023.

### Takeaway
Groww is now the clear leader in retail clients, with about 29% of NSE active clients (Aug 2026), about double Zerodha (~14.8%) and Angel One (~14.6%). Its users skew to Tier 2/3 cities and first-time investors. Angel One says 89% of its new FY26 clients came from Tier 2/3 towns. ### Inferences
- At Groww, MF users (10M) are about 6x F&O users (1.7M), yet F&O earns about 55% of revenue. Most Groww users are long-term MF and SIP investors who earn the company little. They are the natural target for an AI advice or planning layer, and incumbents do not serve them well.

## 6. Tech and infrastructure: how MF orders flow

### Takeaway
MF orders reach AMCs through three paths: exchange platforms (BSE StAR MF, NSE NMF II), the RTAs (CAMS, KFintech) or the MFU/MFCentral utilities, or direct AMC APIs. Zerodha Coin holds units in demat. Groww mainly uses SOA (statement of account, non-demat) folios but now offers a demat option. EOP rules in 2023 formalised how direct-plan platforms can operate.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI's 2013 circular let MF distributors use stock exchange infrastructure to buy and redeem MF units. BSE StAR MF processed 18.47 crore transactions in FY22 (up from 9.38 crore) and recorded 3M transactions in a single day in Apr 2022. These figures are old (2022). — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/2807-what-can-distributors-expect-from-bse-star-mf-platform); [Business Standard 2022](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/bse-s-star-mf-platform-sees-record-3-mn-single-day-transactions-on-monday-122041101201_1.html); [BSE StAR MF](https://www.bseindia.com/Static/Markets/MutualFunds/MFD.aspx)
- Coin holds units in the investor's demat account. Groww uses SOA format and has added an optional demat holding. — [Bajaj Finserv comparison](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/zerodha-vs-groww-mutual-fund); [Zerodha Coin](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/coin/introducing-coin-our-direct-mutual-fund-platform)
- Investors can hold direct units without demat through platforms like Kuvera or MFCentral, in SOA format with zero commission. — [IndiaInvestments wiki](https://www.indiainvestments.wiki/faqs/mfs/best-mutual-fund-app-for-investments/)
- Groww earmarked ₹152.5 Cr of IPO proceeds for cloud infrastructure, which suggests an in-house stack on public cloud. — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/groww-ipo-listing-gmp-cools-to-4)

### Inferences
- An MVP does not need exchange membership. It can use BSE StAR MF through a registered MFD/EOP or a broker partner, plus CAS imports (through CAMS/KFintech, MFCentral or Account Aggregator) for read-only portfolio aggregation. This is how INDmoney tracks external folios via CAS.

### Gaps
- No current (2025/26) BSE StAR MF or NSE NMF II volume figures were found, and no data on which platform uses which rail.
- Whether each incumbent builds MF order routing in-house or uses vendors (for example, BSE StAR MF APIs or Cybrilla/FundsIndia-style middleware) was not verified.

## 7. Weaknesses, regulatory hits, and missing features

### Takeaway
SEBI's F&O measures, phased in from Nov 2024 into 2025, ended Zerodha's growth and cut Angel One's profit by about 22% in FY26. Groww's F&O user share almost halved. Brokers have responded with MTF, commodities, lending and wealth. MF-first apps are weak on monetisation and depth: tracking runs on lagging CAS data and the "advice" is algorithmic. None of the sources found showed any incumbent with strong holistic planning, family portfolios or tax features (this is under-researched; see Gaps).

### Cited Findings
- Zerodha named these hits: higher STT on options, weekly expiries cut to two (one per exchange), a higher limit for Basic Services Demat Accounts (BSDA), removal of exchange transaction-charge rebates (true-to-label, worth about ₹400 Cr to zero), and a general slowdown in trading. It warned that FY26 revenue could fall up to 40% from FY24. Q1 FY26 brokerage fell about 40%, and Zerodha said it was considering ending free equity delivery. — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/bankingfinance/Zerodha-Reports-15percent-Revenue-Drop-in-FY25-Amidst-Regulatory-Changes-Warns-of-Further-Decline/68dcf6c71a9bfbf9a4659406); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/zerodha-brokerage-revenue-drops-40-in-q1-fy26-10516905); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/zerodha-mulls-ending-zero-brokerage-model)
- Kamath said trading activity has been falling since its peak in Sept 2024. — [Lapaas](https://lapaasvoice.com/zerodha-fy26-profit-rises-1-2-to-%e2%82%b94283-crore-brokerage-income-falls-10-7/)
- Inc42 says "Zerodha's diversification clock is ticking". — [Inc42 feature](https://inc42.com/features/zerodhas-diversification-clock-is-ticking/)
- Angel One's FY26 PAT fell 21.9%, described as "a clear scar from the mid-year regulatory disruption" plus IPL costs. F&O concentration of 47% of gross income is flagged as a risk. — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/bankingfinance/Angel-One-FY26-Profit-at-indian-rupee915-Cr-Income-indian-rupee5152-Cr-Adds-69M-Clients/6a32b0cbb6609c8f9dd1a423)
- Groww: F&O user share fell from 18% to 10%. The AMC (₹21.4 Cr loss) and Fisdom (₹10.2 Cr loss) are not yet profitable. — [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-groww-q4-fy26-fo-user-share-decline-customer-shift-mutual-funds-etfs/)
- Paytm Money is losing active customers, and revenue fell 11% in FY25. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/one97-communications-to-invest-%e2%82%b9300-crore-in-broking-entity-paytm-money-79615.htm)
- INDmoney: tracking of external folios refreshes from CAS, not in real time. Its advice is "algorithmic rather than fiduciary". Losses widened in FY25 (cash loss ₹76 Cr). — [Foliyo](https://foliyo.ai/guides/mf-platforms/indmoney-review/); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/indmoneys-revenue-jumps-23x-to-rs-164-cr-in-fy25-10965136)
- ET Money keeps its portfolio review (Genius) behind a paywall. — [Vittsphere](https://one.vittsphere.com/blog/personal-finance/best-ai-personal-finance-platforms-india-2026/)

### Inferences
- The large brokers' revenue depends on regulation, through STT, expiry rules and margin rules. Their MF users are a low-revenue base that their product teams are not focused on serving. This is where an advice-led AI MF product could fit.
- The likely gaps across incumbents are fiduciary personalised advice, goal-based planning, tax-loss harvesting and capital-gains planning, consolidated family portfolios, and proactive "what should I do now" guidance. Only partial evidence for these gaps was found (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No systematic feature matrix was compiled (goal tracking, tax harvesting, family accounts, holistic net worth). From background knowledge, not verified: Kuvera has had family accounts and tax-harvesting tools, INDmoney has net-worth tracking including US stocks, and Groww and Zerodha offer only basic goal/SIP features. This needs hands-on product audits.
- No data was found on complaint rates, outages or SEBI penalties for individual platforms in 2025 and 2026.

## 8. AI features launched (2025 to 2026)

### Takeaway
Incumbent AI has mostly meant read-only connectors to outside chatbots, not native advisers. Zerodha's Kite MCP (May 2025) and Groww MCP (around mid-2025) let users connect accounts to Claude, Cursor and others. ET Money Genius and INDmoney use algorithmic recommendations. Dhan's parent has "Fuzz", an AI insights model. No incumbent was found to have launched a full AI financial adviser registered as an RIA as of Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Zerodha Kite MCP: free connector linking a Zerodha account to AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, Windsurf), which can retrieve holdings including MFs. Launched about May 20 2025. — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/featured/connect-your-zerodha-account-to-ai-assistants-with-kite-mcp); [Medium](https://medium.com/@pranaymessi12345/zerodhas-kite-mcp-when-your-ai-assistant-finally-understands-your-money-8570854b9347)
- Groww MCP: connects a Groww profile to Claude, Cursor or VS Code for portfolio tracking and insights. The page was about 429 days old as of Oct 2026, so it launched around mid-2025. — [Groww updates](https://groww.in/updates/groww-mcp)
- ET Money Genius: scores funds, builds risk-matched portfolios from questionnaire answers, and suggests switches (paid). — [Vittsphere](https://one.vittsphere.com/blog/personal-finance/best-ai-personal-finance-platforms-india-2026/)
- Raise (Dhan's parent): "Fuzz" AI model for contextual and verified financial insights. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/dhan-parent-raise-launches-millions-investment-app-targeting-gen-z/) (via search summary)
- Third-party, unofficial projects combine Groww, Kite and INDmoney into one AI view, some by scraping with Playwright. This shows unmet demand for cross-platform AI portfolio views. — [GitHub Sparker0i](https://github.com/Sparker0i/indian-stock-mcp-agent)
- New AI-first entrants are appearing, for example an "agentic card" from Cruise Money. — [ThePrint/ANI](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/cruise-money-showcases-indias-first-agentic-card-an-ai-money-companion-that-plans-decides-pays-on-the-users-behalf/3050638/)

### Inferences
- Incumbents have treated AI as a developer or power-user add-on (MCP connectors) rather than a core advice experience. The likely reason is SEBI's advice and RIA boundary. This leaves room for an AI-native product built from the start under an RIA licence, or carefully limited to "analysis" under EOP/MFD rules.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm 2026 AI assistant launches inside the Groww, INDmoney, Angel One or Upstox apps; searches returned mostly listicles. Angel One has historically promoted AI and ML features, but this was not verified this session. Check official press releases.
- No SEBI circular specific to AI investment advice was confirmed as of Oct 2026.
