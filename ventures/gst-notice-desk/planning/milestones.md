## M0 Discovery & legal

**Due:** Friday 23 October 2026 (weeks 1-2).

**Goal.** Learn whether CA firms feel a paid pain strongly enough to proceed, sign design partners, and clear the legal gate that lets partner data in.

**Exit criteria**
- [ ] 20 CA interviews logged; at least 8 of 20 confirm a paid pain (HANDOFF phase-0 gate)
- [ ] Notices per client and CA time per notice recorded as interview-based estimates
- [ ] Competitor teardown of Clear, Suvit, Zoho Books, Tally (TallyIra) and Accu Reco filed
- [ ] 3-5 design-partner firms signed under data-sharing terms and an anonymisation protocol (gate G1)
- [ ] Anonymisation tool delivered to partners; first exports received from at least 2 firms
- [ ] Counsel engaged with scope and delivery dates
- [ ] CA advisor appointed; labelling template ready
- [ ] Repository, CI and synthetic data generator running

**If missed.** Fewer than 8 of 20 confirmations: reposition or stop (HANDOFF section 11). Partner exports late: M1 slips to 6 November.

## M1 Prototype

**Due:** Friday 30 October 2026 (week 3; engine work starts in week 1 on synthetic data).

**Goal.** Show that the engine and the drafter work on real anonymised data from 3-5 partners and that the eval harness reports honest numbers.

**Exit criteria**
- [ ] Reconciliation: at least 95% line-level agreement with CA labels on 100 cases; zero wrong auto-accepts in tiers 1-2
- [ ] Extraction: deadline and demand correct on at least 95% of 15-20 real anonymised notices
- [ ] Drafts: every citation resolves to corpus text; CA advisor rates at least 50% of drafts "edit, not rewrite"
- [ ] Per-supplier action list report generated by one command
- [ ] Eval harness runs in one command and in CI
- [ ] Demo given to at least 3 partners; at least 3 of 5 would use the output on a live client
- [ ] Findings memo with measured values and failure list

**If missed.** Recon below 90% on real data fails M1; fix before UI work. M1 may slip to 6 November, using M2 buffer.

## M2 MVP

**Due:** Friday 11 December 2026 (weeks 4-9).

**Goal.** A multi-tenant web app that a CA firm runs unaided for a monthly cycle, with the CA sign-off gate and audit log in place.

**Exit criteria**
- [ ] All P0 stories (CP-1 to CP-5, AA-1 to AA-5) pass the acceptance criteria in the plan, section 5.3
- [ ] Tenant isolation test (AC-9) and eval regression gate (AC-10) green in CI
- [ ] Recon at least 98% line-level agreement on 150 cases; extraction at least 98% on 50 notices; at least 60% of drafts accepted with minor edits; citation precision at least 92% (the 60% and 98% follow HANDOFF section 10; the rest are assumptions)
- [ ] 50 consented notices collected; safety set escalates 100% of cases
- [ ] One pilot-ready firm completes a monthly cycle unaided
- [ ] Security checklist and restore drill done
- [ ] Gates G2 (counsel opinion on drafting versus representation), G3 (processing agreement, deletion flow) and G4 (model-vendor terms) cleared

**If missed.** Pilot starts in shadow mode only; no live-client use until G2 and G3 clear.

## M3 Pilot & decision

**Due:** Friday 29 January 2027 (weeks 10-16).

**Goal.** Run 10-15 firms on live data through two monthly return cycles (November and December data), test price, and decide.

**Exit criteria** (HANDOFF section 11 targets; thresholds are assumptions to confirm at the end of M0)
- [ ] 10-15 firms active; target at least 8 paying (HANDOFF day-90 target: 15 firms active, at least 8 paying)
- [ ] Price test at INR 150, 250 and 400 per GSTIN complete; at least INR 150 accepted (kill trigger: only below INR 75)
- [ ] Recon accuracy at least 99% (pivot trigger: below 95% after fixes)
- [ ] Citation precision at least 95% (trigger: below 90% means evidence and extraction only, no drafting)
- [ ] CA draft acceptance at least 70% (trigger: below 40%)
- [ ] Median time to a CA-ready draft under 30 minutes
- [ ] Weekly use in 3 of 4 weeks per firm; supplier-chase resolution at least 30% within 30 days
- [ ] No missed deadline and no data leak (either pauses the pilot)
- [ ] Gates G6 (terms, liability) and G7 (entity) cleared before charging
- [ ] Decision memo with measured values: go, pivot or stop, recorded on 29 January 2027

**If missed.** Apply the kill and pivot table in HANDOFF section 11.
