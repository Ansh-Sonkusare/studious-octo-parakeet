## Gaps the research does not close

These are repeated from the source files and HANDOFF because they decide what discovery (M0) must answer:
- No official count of 2B-mismatch notices to small taxpayers, and no independent data on CA cost per notice ([unsolved_smb_payments.md](unsolved_smb_payments.md), Problem 3 gaps).
- The 5% versus 20% tolerance claim in one article is unverified, and the applicable section differs by source.
- Clear, Zoho and Vayana products were not surveyed; Zoho Books pricing and features were not researched ([b2b_fintech_gaps.md](b2b_fintech_gaps.md), section 4 gaps).
- ICAI listing criteria and fees are not in the sources, and the 2026 ICAI member count was not found.
- GSP/ASP mechanics and representation rules are background knowledge that counsel must confirm.
- LLM prices come from third-party aggregators; check the vendor's current price page ([tech_feasibility.md](tech_feasibility.md), section 6 gaps).
