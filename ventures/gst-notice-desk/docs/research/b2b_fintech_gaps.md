# B2B and "Picks-and-Shovels" AI Opportunities in Indian Finance (as of Oct 2026)

Research scope: AI products sold to intermediaries and businesses (MFDs, RIAs, RAs, PMS/AIF, CAs, brokers, AMCs, banks/NBFCs, MSMEs), not consumers. Research date: 7 Oct 2026. Method: ~15 web searches; many figures are secondhand (trade press or aggregators), and they are flagged where unverified.

## 1. Intermediary buyer pools (MFDs, RIAs, RAs, PMS/AIF, CAs): size, current software, and what is missing

### Takeaway
MFDs are by far the largest and fastest-growing pool of professional buyers: about 3.4–3.5 lakh ARN/EUIN holders, of which about 1.79 lakh are individuals. Their platforms are already crowded with free, commission-funded tools that are adding AI quickly (Wealthy, AssetPlus, ZFunds' ZIVA, Prudent's AI tools, all launched or funded between late 2025 and mid 2026). That leaves little room for a generic "AI copilot for MFDs". The pools that pay subscription fees are small but sit under heavy regulation: about 1,000 RIAs, about 1,500+ RAs, and 515–530+ PMS managers. That pattern favours compliance and documentation tooling over distribution tooling.

### Inferences
- Platforms like AssetPlus, Wealthy, ZFunds, Prudent/FundzBazar and NJ give MFDs software for free in exchange for routing transactions and commissions. A standalone paid "AI for MFDs" product therefore competes with free bundled AI. A small team should instead (a) sell a platform-agnostic layer that works across whichever back-office tools the MFD already uses (Investwell, Wealth Elite and others), (b) sell to the platforms themselves as a white-label or API vendor, or (c) target the about 3,350 top MFDs and large RIAs/PMS that already pay for software.
- Workflows these sources show as not yet commoditised: compliance-checked client communications (WhatsApp/email), suitability and rationale documentation tied to the RIA/RA record-keeping duty, multi-platform consolidated reporting (MF plus stocks, PMS/AIF, insurance and FDs), and SIF/new-product education for the 8,371 SIF distributors.
- The RIA and RA pools are small (about 1,000 and about 1,500) but every member is obligated to keep records, disclose AI use and comply with the advertisement code. Revenue ceiling at ₹20–50k a year per firm: about 2,500 firms × ₹30k ≈ ₹7.5 crore a year. That is viable for a small team as a wedge but not a large business on its own. This arithmetic is my own illustrative estimate.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 counts were found for insurance POSPs/agents, sub-brokers/APs, ICAI members (the brief's figure of about 4 lakh is unverified here), tax practitioners, or family offices.
- No current public pricing was found for Wealth Elite, NJ, Kuvera Pro or FundzBazar's partner tools. Investwell's pricing is dated.
- No Reddit/Twitter MFD complaint threads were sampled because of the tool budget. Evidence of buyer pain rests on regulator statements and vendor positioning, not first-hand MFD voice.

## 2. Regtech/compliance AI: advertisement code, AI disclosure, CSCRF, record-keeping

### Takeaway
Regulatory change in 2025–26 creates time-bound compliance work for a few thousand SEBI entities. SEBI's board approved a Common Advertisement Code on 24 Sept 2026 that swaps prior approval for 24-hour post-publication reporting (except celebrity/finfluencer endorsements). RIAs/RAs have had to disclose AI use since Jan 2025. CSCRF imposes annual VAPT/cyber audit cycles with the next filing due 30 Nov 2026. A small team could build an "ad-classification, reporting and archive" tool plus a CSCRF-lite compliance kit for small regulated entities. No dominant Indian vendor was identified for either.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI issued the Common Advertisement Code (CAC) consultation on 23 June 2026. It covers brokers, DPs, IAs, RAs, online bond platforms, PMS and MFs/AMCs. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/fund-management-reits/1826178/sebis-proposal-for-a-common-advertisement-code-financial-advertisements-under-the-regulatory-lens); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-proposes-common-advertisement-code-eases-rules-for-regulated-entities/80870.html)
- The SEBI board approved the CAC at its 215th meeting on 24 Sept (2026). Most ads move from prior approval to post-issuance reporting within 24 hours. Celebrity endorsements keep pre-clearance. — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/sebi-relaxes-advertising-norms-keeps-celebrity-endorsements-under-prior-approval-158597.html); [IndianTelevision](https://indiantelevision.com/regulators/sebi-approves-common-advertising-code-allows-celebrity-promotion-by-regulated-entities/)
- The draft defines "celebrity" to include finfluencers with more than 500,000 followers on at least one platform, OTT/reality-TV names and AI avatars. Commentary notes that unpaid finfluencers stay outside its reach. — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/sebi-wants-one-ad-code-celebrities-back-finfluencers-on-hook/)
- Under CAC, firms must show that every ad was correctly classified, compliant when issued, reported on time and supportable on later examination. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/fund-management-reits/1826178/sebis-proposal-for-a-common-advertisement-code-financial-advertisements-under-the-regulatory-lens)
- The final notified text and effective date were not confirmed. The draft proposed a six-month transition after notification. — same Mondaq source
- Research reports are not treated as ads unless they promote the RA's own products or services. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/research-reports-are-not-ads-clarifies-sebi-46112.htm)
- Since the guidelines of 8 January 2025, RIAs and RAs must disclose to clients the extent of AI tools used in advice and research. — [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/news/sebi-issues-guideline-for-research-analysts-investment-advisers); [FoxMandal](https://foxmandal.in/news/ras-and-ias-to-take-on-additional-responsibilities/)
- SEBI has proposed restricting intermediaries' association with unregistered finfluencers. — [FoxMandal](https://foxmandal.in/News/sebi-proposes-to-restrict-association-of-intermediaries-with-unregistered-finfluencers/)
- CSCRF: the original deadlines were 1 Jan 2025 for existing entities and 1 Apr 2025 for new ones, later extended to 30 June 2025 for most REs (brokers, AMCs, PMS, IAs). No extension applied to MIIs, KRAs or Qualified RTAs. — [Zeron](https://zeron.one/sebi-grants-deadline-extension-for-cscrf-compliance-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)
- The current NSE cycle for self-certification, small, mid-size and qualified REs: VAPT by a CERT-In auditor by 30 June 2026, the report after IT Committee approval by 31 July 2026, and the action-taken/revalidation report by 30 Nov 2026. — [NSE circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INSP74185.pdf) (listed in search results; specific circular number not fully verified)
- Brokers with fewer than 1,000 clients and below ₹1,000 crore in volume are exempt. Small REs may keep IT asset inventories in Excel. — [SEBI clarification, 30 Apr 2025 (APMI copy)](https://apmiindia.org/storagebox/images/Circulars/Clarifications on CSCRF for SEBI Regulated Entities - 30th April'25.pdf); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/securities/1648184/navigating-sebis-cybersecurity-and-cyber-resilience-framework-key-clarifications-for-sebi-regulated-entities)
- Generic Indian VAPT price ranges for 2026 (not CSCRF-specific): external network ₹30k–₹1L, internal ₹60k–₹2L, web app ₹40k–₹1.5L. — [TCSA](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/vapt-cost-india-2026)
- Vendors currently pitching CSCRF services include Ogma, Qualysec, SecurityHQ, CyberSigma and Zeron. — [Ogma](https://ogma.in/blog/sebi-cscrf-compliance-vapt-and-cyber-audit-requirements-for-brokers-exchanges-and-mutual-funds); [CyberSigma case study](https://cybersigmacs.com/case-study/stockbroker-sebi-cscrf-compliance-programme/)
- OnFinance AI markets "BFSI AI agents for SEBI & RBI" compliance, risk and audit. This is promotional and was not verified. — [OnFinance newsletter](https://onfinanceainewsletter.beehiiv.com/p/bfsi-ai-agents-for-sebi-rbi-transforming-compliance-risk-and-audit-47d1)
- Commercial KYC/identity infra is a large, proven B2B market. Perfios reported FY25 operating revenue of ₹669.5 Cr and profit of ₹104.3 Cr ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/perfios-fy25-profit-soars-46-to-₹104-cr-revenue-crosses-₹700-cr/)). IDfy reported ₹188.5 Cr revenue and ₹7.8 Cr profit, unaudited ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-idfy-posts-rs-188-cr-revenue-in-fy25-while-maintaining-profitability-9512349)); multiples.vc gives ₹186 Cr and ₹1.6 Cr profit. Signzy reported ₹151.3 Cr revenue and a loss of about ₹32.6 Cr ([Inc42 Datalabs](https://inc42.com/company/signzy/financials/)).

### Inferences
- The CAC shift from pre-approval to "report within 24h + prove compliance" turns compliance into a logging and evidence problem. An AI tool that classifies each social post, reel or WhatsApp broadcast (ad or not, celebrity or not), checks it against the code, auto-files the 24h report and archives evidence fits small teams. Buyers are about 1,000 RIAs, about 1,500 RAs, 515+ PMS, brokers and APs, plus MFDs if AMFI adopts similar norms. Timing is good: a six-month transition is likely to fall in roughly H1 2027 if the code is notified soon (an inference from the draft's transition window).
- AI-use disclosure (Jan 2025) plus RIA/RA record-keeping creates demand for an "advice audit trail": recorded calls, transcripts, risk-profile-to-recommendation rationale and suitability logs, generated automatically.
- KYC/AML is a crowded market (Perfios, IDfy, Signzy each have ₹150–670 Cr in revenue). A small team should not try to compete there head-on.
- CSCRF for small and self-certification REs is today served by VAPT/audit service firms. A self-serve "CSCRF-in-a-box" (policy templates, asset inventory, evidence collection, IT committee pack, reminders for the 30 Nov filing) is a plausible productised SaaS. Willingness to pay is likely anchored to VAPT fees of ₹30k–₹2L a year.

### Gaps
- No Indian vendor specialising in AI ad-compliance for RIAs/MFDs was found, and no pricing for one. This is an absence in the search results, not proof that the market is empty.
- No data was found on the number of REs in each CSCRF category (self-certification, small, mid-size), so the buyer count for a CSCRF tool is unknown.
- Call-recording/audit tools for brokers and RIAs (players and pricing) were not researched within the budget.

## 3. AI for AMCs, brokers, banks: voice agents, support, research and earnings-call tools

### Takeaway
Enterprise BFSI voice AI is a real revenue category in India, now dominated by well-funded players. Gnani.ai reports about ₹54 Cr revenue and is EBITDA-positive; Sarvam reports ₹45 Cr FY26 revenue, became a $1.5B unicorn, and won the Mahindra Finance deployment. A small team should avoid horizontal voice AI. Equity research AI (concall summaries, filings) is being contested by Tijori (backed by Zerodha) and Trendlyne (which already sells an MCP data plan at ₹299 a month). Remaining gaps are niche: vertical workflows for mid-size AMCs, PMS and brokers.

### Cited Findings
- Gnani.ai raised a $10M first tranche of its Series B in March 2026, led by Aavishkaar Capital with Info Edge Ventures. It reports revenue of ₹53.87 Cr (fiscal year not stated) and is EBITDA-profitable. Clients include 3 of the top 5 Indian banks, 5 top NBFCs and the top 3 insurers, among them Bank of Baroda, HDFC Bank, IDFC First and Muthoot. — [Business Today, Mar 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/story/gnaniai-raises-10-million-in-funding-from-aavishkaar-capital-to-scale-global-voice-ai-push-523218-2026-03-31)
- Gnani claims it helped a bank collect over $400M in overdue EMIs using voice agents (company claim). — [Pulse 2.0 interview](https://pulse2.com/gnani-ai-profile-ganesh-gopalan-interview/)
- Sarvam reported FY26 revenue of ₹45.1 Cr (unaudited, shared by HCLTech), up from ₹1.5 Cr in FY25. It raised $234M at a $1.5B post-money valuation with HCLTech as lead strategic investor (June 2026). Conversational agents were about 80% of its roughly $12M ARR in an earlier estimate. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/sarvam-becomes-indias-130th-unicorn-after-raising-234-mn/)
- At GFF 2026, Mahindra Finance expanded its partnership with Sarvam for voice AI across sales, collections and frontline engagement in rural and semi-urban markets, calling it "one of the largest voice AI deployments in Indian financial services". — [Inc42, GFF 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/gff-2026-fintech-ai-partnerships-take-the-centre-stage-on-day-2/)
- A competitor blog claims voice AI pricing in India has fallen sharply (commercial, biased source). — [Caller.Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/gnani-ai-alternatives-india-2026)
- Tijori raised $5M from Zerodha (late 2025) to move toward enterprise customers. Retail pricing is about ₹500 a month and enterprise up to ₹5,000 a month. Its "Call Monitor" auto-summarises earnings calls within minutes and flags inconsistencies between management commentary and filings; launch status was unverified. — [YourStory, Nov 2025](https://www.yourstory.com/2025/11/tijori-raises-5m-from-zerodha-to-expand-its-ai-product-suite); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/zerodha-invests-5-mn-in-tijori-10817854)
- Trendlyne sells MCP plans that connect its data to AI tools like Claude. Pro is ₹299 a month with tool-call limits and covers quarterly results, investor presentations, earnings calls and annual reports. Pro/Max are for individual use only; Enterprise is "contact us". — [Trendlyne MCP plans](https://trendlyne.com/subscription/mcp/plans/)

### Inferences
- Horizontal vernacular voice for banks and NBFCs is taken: Gnani, Sarvam, Yellow.ai, Skit.ai and others, with bank-grade references. Small-team openings are thin layers on top, such as QA/compliance auditing of collection calls (RBI fair-practice and recovery-agent conduct), or voice agents for underserved mid-market buyers (small NBFCs, cooperative banks, MFIs, small brokers) that big vendors do not chase.
- Equity research AI is commoditising fast, with Tijori, Trendlyne, Screener and broker-built tools. Differentiated opportunities could be (a) compliance-ready RA report drafting (report templates with SEBI RA disclosures and the AI-use disclosure built in) or (b) PMS/AIF investor reporting and factsheet generation, given SEBI's push against exaggerated PMS performance claims.

### Gaps
- Yellow.ai, Skit.ai and Haptik BFSI revenue and pricing were not researched.
- No AMC-specific AI procurement data (budgets, RFPs) was found.
- Screener, Tikr and MarketSmith India pricing and AI features were not checked.

## 4. SMB/MSME finance: GST, accounting, Tally ecosystem, collections and lending

### Takeaway
Tally shipped its own AI (TallyIra / "Docs by Ira", TallyPrime 7.1, June 2026) for document-to-voucher entry, including WhatsApp uploads. That directly erodes the most obvious "AI add-on for Tally" wedge. CA-facing automation tools such as Suvit (GST reconciliation, Tally automation, distributed through ICAI's member-benefit portal at a 50% discount) show CAs as an accessible channel. MSME-facing bookkeeping apps remain loss-making on modest revenue: Vyapar had about ₹69–77 Cr revenue and a ₹63 Cr loss in FY25; Khatabook had ₹102.7 Cr revenue and a ₹116 Cr loss in FY24. Selling to CAs, who serve many MSMEs each, looks more efficient than selling directly to MSMEs.

### Cited Findings
- TallyPrime Release 7.1 introduced TallyIra, Tally's first built-in AI capability. Its first feature, "Docs by Ira", reads business documents uploaded from desktop, mobile or WhatsApp and drafts GST-compliant accounting entries. Coverage dates to about 23 June 2026. — [CXO Digital Pulse](https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/?p=39293); [Tally blog](https://tallysolutions.com/accounting/future-of-ai-in-accounting-and-finance-trends-businesses-should-watch/)
- Tally had earlier piloted a conversational engine for natural-language business queries (timing unconfirmed). — [Tally (AWS dev mirror)](https://awsdevint.tallysolutions.com/technology/empowering-msmes-through-ai/)
- TallyPrime 5.0 added a consolidated in-product GST interface, which Tally said could save MSMEs 60–70% of GST filing time. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-news/tally-solutions-launches-tallyprime-50-with-seamless-multilingual-experience-1.1726137077611)
- Third-party AI add-ons market invoice matching, ledger classification, bank reconciliation and GST verification for Tally, for example AI Accountant. — [AI Accountant](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/ultimate-guide-tally-prime-automation)
- A separate YC company called "Tally" (tallyhq) builds AI agents for accounting firms focused on audit. It is not Tally Solutions. — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tallyhq)
- Suvit offers GSTR-1 and GSTR-2A/2B reconciliation, eligible ITC reports, GST health reports and filing. It is listed on ICAI's member benefits portal at a 50% discount, with free Tally/bank integration setup. It claims 25K+ users and 4K+ CAs on ICAI's listing but "10,000+ CAs" on its own site (conflicting vendor claims). — [ICAI benefits portal](https://bs.icai.org/suvit-2/); [Suvit](https://www.suvit.io/post/accounting-with-ai-for-cas)
- ICAI's member benefits programme also lists Accu Reco, a cloud GST ITC reconciliation tool. — [ICAI CMP](https://betacmp.icai.org/?p=1502)
- Vyapar FY25: operating revenue about ₹69 Cr (+53%) and a net loss of ₹63 Cr per Entrackr's RoC filings, or ₹77.1 Cr per Inc42. About 90% of income is from software licences; employee costs are 72% of expenses. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/vyapar-posts-rs-63-cr-loss-in-fy25-cash-reserve-fades-93-10819211); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/vyapar-app/financials/)
- Khatabook FY24: revenue ₹102.7 Cr and net loss ₹116.2 Cr. Its revenue is mainly not subscriptions (UPI/QR fees, Pro subscriptions, lending, services). — [YourStory](https://cwv.yourstory.com/2024/11/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-clocks-rs-1027-cr-revenue-cuts-losses-7-in-fy24)

### Inferences
- "AI data entry into Tally" is now a native Tally feature. Remaining add-on space covers exception handling and reconciliation (bank, GSTR-2B vs books, vendor ledgers), multi-client CA practice dashboards, notice handling (GST/IT notice reading and draft replies), and month-end close checklists. Those are CA-practice workflows, not MSME self-serve ones.
- The CA channel is efficient. One CA firm serves dozens to hundreds of MSME clients, ICAI runs a vendor discount marketplace, and the GST and ITR deadline calendar creates recurring urgency.
- MSME self-serve bookkeeping apps show weak willingness to pay, with heavy losses at ₹70–100 Cr revenue. A small team should avoid direct-to-MSME freemium.
- AA- and GST-data underwriting for MSME lending is dominated by infra players like Perfios (₹669 Cr revenue). A small team's angle would be lender-side workflow (credit memo drafting, bank statement anomaly narratives) sold to small NBFCs, rather than raw data.

### Gaps
- The ICAI member count for 2026 was not found; the brief's figure of about 4 lakh is unverified. The number of tax practitioners and GST practitioners was also not found.
- Open, RazorpayX, Zoho Books and ClearTax AI features and pricing were not researched within the budget.
- No data was found on AA-based MSME lending volumes or on cash-flow forecasting/collections startups.

## 5. Data/infra APIs and MCP servers for Indian finance

### Takeaway
An AI-ready Indian finance data layer is emerging but fragmented. Trendlyne sells MCP access, and there are community MCP servers for Zerodha Kite, Groww and Dhan, plus multi-broker servers. No first-party Groww or Dhan MCP was found, and no verified, licensed MF-holdings/fundamentals/filings MCP for enterprise use was identified. A licensed, compliance-safe data plus MCP layer (MF portfolios, AMFI NAVs, corporate filings, SEBI orders) for intermediaries and builders is a plausible picks-and-shovels play, but data licensing (exchange/AMFI terms) is the key risk.

### Cited Findings
- Remote Kite (Zerodha) MCP: about 22 tools (holdings, positions, quotes, history, orders, GTT), authenticated via Zerodha login with 2FA, mostly read-only except GTT. — [DEV Community guide by Tapetide (vendor-authored)](https://dev.to/govind_sisara/indian-stock-market-mcp-the-best-mcp-servers-for-nse-bse-data-2026-p28)

- Community aranjan/kite-mcp has 14 tools and low adoption. — [Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/aranjan/kite-mcp)
- Multi-broker MCPs cover Groww, Zerodha, INDmoney, Angel One, Upstox and 5paisa (a Windows executable "indian-broker-mcp"). A separate project unifies Kite, Groww and Dhan. No first-party Groww or Dhan MCP was found. — [LobeHub](https://lobehub.com/mcp?q=groww); [LobeHub Dhan](https://lobehub.com/mcp?q=dhan-api)

### Inferences
- Existing MCPs are broker/trading-centric and aimed at retail. A B2B gap exists for an intermediary-grade data layer: CAS (consolidated account statement) parsing, MF scheme portfolios and overlap, AMFI/SEBI circular search, and SEBI order/registration lookups (for example, verifying an RIA/RA or detecting a cancelled registration). Vendors such as MFD platforms, compliance tools and wealth apps could consume this via API or MCP.
- SEBI registration data (many RA cancellations in 2026) plus finfluencer rules create demand for a "registration verification API" usable by platforms vetting partners or influencers. This is an inference; no existing vendor was checked.

### Gaps

- Indian fundamentals API vendors were not researched: CMOTS, Accord Fintech/ACE Equity, Capitaline, Prowess (CMIE), Screener's lack of an API.

## 6. Willingness to pay and sales cycles by buyer type; Indian B2B fintech SaaS revenues

### Takeaway
Evidence of willingness to pay is strongest for enterprise BFSI infra (Perfios ₹669.5 Cr, IDfy ₹188 Cr, Signzy ₹151 Cr, Gnani about ₹54 Cr, Sarvam ₹45 Cr) and for regulation-driven spend (VAPT/CSCRF ₹30k–₹2L per test). It is weakest for MSMEs and for MFDs, whose tools are commission-subsidised and free. Mid-tier regulated professionals (RIAs, RAs, PMS, CAs) pay modest subscriptions: Investwell's historic ₹25k base, Tijori enterprise up to ₹5k a month, Trendlyne MCP ₹299 a month.
