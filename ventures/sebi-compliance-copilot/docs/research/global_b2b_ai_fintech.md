# Global B2B AI Fintech (sell to the advisor / accountant / institution): category map, traction, pricing, and emerging-market transferability (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Most figures are company-reported or from trade press and press-release aggregators; where sources conflict, this is flagged. Search tooling returned many secondary aggregators (Sacra-style, multiples.vc, vcbacked, etc.); primary press releases are preferred and noted where available.

## 1. AI for financial advisors / wealth managers (notetakers + CRM automation, tax-return analysis, planning, compliance, proposals)

### Takeaway
Advisor AI is the most proven "sell to the advisor" category: Jump (27k advisors, $105M raised) and Zocks (5,000+ firms, $65M raised) scaled to Series B in 2026 on a ~$60-$120 per advisor per month notetaker wedge, then expanded toward "AI operating system" for the practice. Pricing is per-seat SaaS; distribution through broker-dealer/custodian vendor programs (LPL, Osaic) is the key unlock, and CRM vendors bundling notetakers (Wealthbox at $75/user/mo CRM) create pricing pressure.

### Cited Findings
**Market size (US)**
- BLS counted about 326,000 personal financial advisor jobs in 2024 (~270,000 wage-and-salary); a secondary site attributes ~283,000 working advisors to Cerulli (unverified against primary); FINRA lists 723,731 registered individuals (all securities registrants, not just advisors) — [Walnut Invest summary](https://walnutinvest.com/stats/financial-advisor-statistics); [Kitces on headcount/TAM](https://www.kitces.com/blog/financial-advisor-headcount-total-addressable-market-tam-technology-hiring-growth/); [PlanAdviser](https://www.planadviser.com/the-pool-of-advisers-continues-to-shrink/). The oft-cited "~300k" is consistent with older Cerulli coverage; headcount is flat (~+0.2% over a decade per the secondary source).
- Number of US RIA firms hit a record 16,544 in 2025 (+674 firms) — [ThinkAdvisor, June 3 2026](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/2026/06/03/number-of-rias-sets-new-record-report/)

**Meeting notetakers / CRM automation**
- Jump: $80M Series B led by Insight Partners, announced Feb 19-20, 2026; total raised $105M (incl. $20M Series A Feb 2025); 27,000 advisors in under two years, 2,000+ new advisors per month, "nearly 1 in 10" US advisors; ~200 employees (vs 40 at Series A); est. $12T client assets at using firms; investors include F-Prime, Allianz Life Ventures, TIAA Ventures, Battery, Citi Ventures — [WealthManagement.com](https://wealthmanagement.com/artificial-intelligence/jump_secures_series_b); [Fintech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/02/20/jump-secures-80m-series-b-to-scale-ai-for-advisors/). The article states no ARR or pricing; no Jump ARR found.
- Jump distribution: in LPL Financial's vendor affinity program; Jump and Zocks both added as official vendors by Osaic (11,000 advisors) — [Financial Planning](https://www.financial-planning.com/news/ai-tools-jump-and-zocks-gain-funding-and-advisor-fans); [WealthManagement.com Series A](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/artificial-intelligence/popular-ai-startup-for-advisors-jump-raises-20m)
- Zocks: $45M Series B (Jan 26, 2026) co-led by Lightspeed and QED; total $65M after $13.8M Series A (Mar 2025); claims 5,000+ financial firms, enterprise customers Ameritas, Carson Group, Kestra, Osaic; one report says revenue grew 8x YoY (unverified); company claims >10 hours/week saved — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/01/258386-zocks-an-ai-financial-advisor-platform-raises-45m-series-b/); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/zocks-45-million-series-b/amp/)
- Zeplyn: $3M seed (Nov 2024) led by Leo Capital, founded by ex-Google engineers; claims 10-12 hrs/week saved — [WealthManagement.com](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/technology/zeplyn-ai-note-taking-assistant-advisors-announces-3m-seed-funding). No later round found.
- Finmate AI: AI meeting intelligence for advisors, claims at least 5 hrs/week saved; funding not confirmed (a Caplight banner about a "$16M Series A" is Caplight's own announcement, not Finmate's) — [WealthTender](https://wealthtender.com/service-providers/finmate-ai/); [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/finmate)
- Adoption: 43% of advisors reported using AI notetakers in the 2026 T3/Inside Information survey; tracked notetaker solutions grew from 1 to 14 (secondary source, Rework) — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-financial-advisors-2026)
- Kitces (Mar 17, 2025): adoption highest among solo advisors, ~1/3 lower in 2-3 person teams, comparable for 5-person teams; advisors with most comprehensive plans use notetakers at ~4x rate; industry-specific tools (Jump, Zocks) lead satisfaction; generic Fathom/Fireflies/Otter and Zoom AI Companion gained most adoption; Zoom AI Companion highest adoption but lowest satisfaction; industry-specific notetakers cost "$60-$80/month or more" — [Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/ai-notetakers-client-meeting-for-financial-advisors-adoption-satisfaction-trends-research-productivity/)
- Pricing (sources disagree): Jump ~$100/advisor/mo (Rework) or $100-$120/advisor/mo = ~$1,200-$1,440/yr (WealthManagement.com via search summary) or $75-$175/user/mo (Fast.io); Zocks $67/user/mo annual Essentials (Rework) or $800 / $1,300 per year metered by meetings (WealthManagement.com); Zeplyn ~$120/advisor/mo — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-financial-advisors-2026); [Fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/best-ai-for-financial-advisors-2026/); [WealthManagement.com on Fireflies](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/artificial-intelligence/fireflies-ai-throws-down-the-ai-note-taking-gauntlet)
- Pricing pressure: Wealthbox CRM ($75/user/mo) built-in notetaker expected to be priced below standalone tools — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/ai-notetakers-roll-out-new-capabilities-to-defend-through-expansion/267863)

**Tax-return analysis for planners**
- Holistiplan: claims ~39% share of tax planning software category in the 2026 T3 survey (Income Lab, a competitor, cites 38.92% share and 50,000+ users); passed 20,000 advisors in June 2023; late-2024 repricing nearly doubled cost, e.g., $1,999/yr for 75-household plan vs $1,125 for first 75 returns on FP Alpha Tax (Kitces); older tiers $449 (50 returns) to $30,000 (5,000 returns). No funding/ARR found — [Holistiplan news](https://www.holistiplan.com/news); [Kitces Feb 2025](https://www.kitces.com/blog/the-latest-in-financial-advisortech-february-2025-news-fp-alpha-right-capital-holistiplan-tax-return-extraction/); [Income Lab](https://incomelaboratory.com/holistiplan-alternative/)

**Compliance of communications / marketing**
- Smarsh launched an AI Assistant for Professional Archive (Mar 11, 2025) aimed at RIAs, BDs and smaller regulated firms; a Noise Reduction Agent claims up to 60% fewer alerts (vendor claim); 2026 AI Insights Report says regulators moving from experimentation to enforcement — [Smarsh PR](https://www.smarsh.com/press-release/smarsh-redefines-industry-standards-with-new-compliance-ai-for-small-and-medium-sized-firms); [BusinessWire May 2026](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260507445611/en/Smarsh-Advances-Compliance-with-AI-Technologies-That-Cut-Noise-and-Expose-Risk-Earlier)
- Red Oak (marketing review for FINRA-regulated firms, with an AI Review module) announced a 2026 combination with MirrorWeb — [Red Oak summary](https://yespress.io/red-oak.md). Global Relay: no primary AI source found.

### Inferences
- The wedge that won: a vertical notetaker that writes CRM-ready notes and forms (Jump/Zocks beat generic Zoom/Fathom on satisfaction), then bundles into broker-dealer/custodian affinity programs. Distribution through enterprise channels (LPL, Osaic) matters more than product differences.
- Unit economics: at ~$1,200-$1,440/advisor/yr and 27,000 advisors, Jump's implied revenue run-rate could be on the order of tens of $M, but this is my arithmetic on a mixed user base (not all pay full price) and not a reported ARR.
- Single-feature notetaking is commoditizing (CRM bundling, Zoom/Microsoft free tiers); Series B money is explicitly going to expand scope. A small team entering today needs a niche (e.g., regional compliance format) rather than generic notes.

### Gaps
- No verified ARR for Jump, Zocks, Zeplyn, Finmate, Holistiplan, FP Alpha, RightCapital; Range's AI product, portfolio-proposal generation and FP Alpha/RightCapital AI details were not researched in depth.
- No primary Global Relay AI source; no Red Oak pricing.
- Kitces AdvisorTech map/2026 research not retrievable in full; vendor market share charts unreadable.

## 2. AI for accountants / tax pros and SMB finance (bookkeeping/CFO, AP/AR, tax prep, audit)

### Takeaway
2026 saw billion-dollar rounds for AI-native accounting: Rillet ($100M Series C at $1B, Aug 2026), Basis ($100M Series B at $1.15B, Feb 2026), with Campfire (>$100M raised by late 2025) and Black Ore / Juno in AI tax prep. The winners sell to accounting firms (Basis, Black Ore, Juno) or mid-market finance teams replacing NetSuite (Rillet, Campfire). Pricing is mostly undisclosed/enterprise.

### Cited Findings
- Rillet: $100M Series C at $1B valuation led by ICONIQ with a16z, Sequoia, Bain Capital Ventures, Battery (Aug 17-18, 2026; sources differ on day); total raised ~$208.5M; seed $13.5M (Jul 2024), Series A $25M (May 2025, Sequoia), Series B $70M (Aug 2025); one source says 600+ customers incl. Neuralink and Mercor — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/node/10359); [raising.fi](https://raising.fi/news/rillet-series-c-august-2026); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/rillet/). Founding year inconsistently reported.
- Basis: $100M Series B at $1.15B led by Accel with GV, Lloyd Blankfein, Khosla (Feb 24, 2026); total ~$138M ($3.6M seed Sept 2023, $34M Series A Dec 2024 led by Khosla); company says used by ~30% of top 25 and 20% of top 150 accounting firms; claims first AI agent to autonomously complete an end-to-end 1065 partnership return; Khosla claims 20-50% efficiency gains (investor characterization) — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260224020999/en/Basis-Raises-$100M-at-a-$1.15B-Valuation-as-Accounting-Firms-Adopt-End-to-End-Agents-Across-Accounting,-Tax,-and-Audit); [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/basis-raises-100m-series-b)
- Campfire (AI-native ERP): $35M Series A led by Accel (TechCrunch dated Jun 30, 2025; Campfire's blog dated Aug 27, 2025), then $65M Series B led by Accel and Ribbit about 12 weeks later (>$100M total), claiming 10x YTD revenue growth; no 2026 round or ARR found — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tiny-ai-erp-startup-campfire-is-winning-so-many-startups-from-netsuite-accel-led-a-35m-series-a); [Campfire blog](https://campfire.ai/blog/campfire-65-million-series-b-co-led-by-accel-and-ribbit)
- Black Ore: $60M launch round (Nov 2023; a16z and Oak HC/FT co-led); moved Tax Autopilot out of early access on Apr 29, 2026 with 75 firms chosen from a waitlist of ~4,000 (another source ~3,700), incl. 40% of top-20 CPA firms — [Lex Substack](https://lex.substack.com/p/fintech-black-ore-an-ai-automating); [Street Insider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/Black+Ore+Launches+Tax+Autopilot+for+Broad+Availability/26390608.html)
- Juno: $12M seed (Apr 9, 2026) led by Bonfire Ventures; claims 500 firms (5-100 accountants each) as early adopters; automates "90% of busy work" (company claim); HQ reported as San Diego or San Francisco; one aggregator's $4.1M figure conflicts — [CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/04/13/juno-raises-12m-seed-to-scale-ai-tax-preparation-platform-that-automates-90-of-busy-work/181502/)
- Pilot: total raised reported variously as $150M, $158M+, $161.3M; announced an "AI Accountant" on Feb 4, 2026 (directory listing) — [TechCrunch tag](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2131054); [Clay](https://clay.com/dossier/pilotcom-funding)
- Digits: last found round Series C $65M (Mar 2022, ~$565M valuation); a competitor (Puzzle) claims Digits sold its bookkeeping service business in 2024 (verify) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/digits-2/financials); [Puzzle vs Digits](https://puzzle.io/blog/puzzle-vs-digits). No Puzzle funding/ARR found.

### Inferences
- Two models are working: (a) "services-as-software" for accounting firms (Basis, Black Ore, Juno), and (b) AI-native ERP for startups (Rillet, Campfire). Both avoid direct SMB-owner sales, which has higher churn.
- Valuation multiples are very high on thin disclosed ARR; Campfire/Rillet/Basis hit scale in ~2-3 years of founding, suggesting capital is chasing the "accounting firm labor shortage" narrative.

### Gaps
- No list pricing for Basis, Rillet, Campfire, Digits, Puzzle, Pilot, April, Filed; no ARR for any (except relative growth claims). AP/AR automation (e.g., Ramp, Bill) and audit-AI vendors were not researched. April and Filed not found in searches.

## 5. Proven abroad but nascent in emerging markets: India, SEA, MENA, LatAm (and regional incumbents)

### Takeaway
Equivalent "advisor AI" and "AI for accountants" products are largely absent or early in these markets, but the buyer pools differ: India has ~3.4 lakh mutual fund distributors and 400k+ CAs but only ~1,000 SEBI-registered investment advisers; Brazil and Colombia show early AI-accounting rounds ($1M-$10M); SEA and MENA are thin on B2B AI-for-professionals with 2026 evidence limited to Vietnam's Finan and MENA fintech generally.

## 6. Sales cycles, ACVs, and traits of small-team winners

### Takeaway
Primary evidence on sales cycles and ACV is largely missing from sources reviewed. Observable patterns: self-serve or low-touch per-seat pricing for advisors (~$1,000-$1,500/yr), enterprise/channel deals for BDs, and fast time-to-revenue for agentic "replace a labor workflow" startups.

### Cited Findings
- Advisor seat ACV: Jump ~$1,200-$1,440/advisor/yr; Zocks $800-$1,300/yr (meeting-metered) or $67/mo; Holistiplan $1,999/yr for 75 households — see Section 1 sources.
- Jump grew from 3 co-founders (Feb 2024) to ~200 employees and 27,000 advisors in <2 years with 2,000+ adds/month — [WealthManagement.com](https://wealthmanagement.com/artificial-intelligence/jump_secures_series_b)
- Black Ore ran a two-year early-access program with a ~4,000-firm waitlist before general availability (Apr 2026), picking 75 firms — [Street Insider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/Black+Ore+Launches+Tax+Autopilot+for+Broad+Availability/26390608.html)
- Campfire reported 10x revenue growth in the year to its Series B, described as winning customers away from NetSuite — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tiny-ai-erp-startup-campfire-is-winning-so-many-startups-from-netsuite-accel-led-a-35m-series-a)
- Cara reached seven-figure ARR in 7 months with 80% organic growth (company-reported) — [TamRadar](https://www.tamradar.com/funding-rounds/cara-seed-8m)
- Kitces: adoption concentrated among solo advisors who cannot delegate; advisors spend >1 hr on prep/follow-up per hour of meeting — [Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/ai-notetakers-client-meeting-for-financial-advisors-adoption-satisfaction-trends-research-productivity/)

### Inferences
- Winning traits: (1) vertical depth (CRM write-back, compliance-ready notes) vs generic tools; (2) channel distribution via custodians/broker-dealers; (3) expansion from one wedge to a platform after traction; (4) targeting the solo/small-team buyer who has no one to delegate to (highest adoption).
- Advisor-seat deals are likely short-cycle (days to weeks self-serve) while enterprise/BD and bank deals are months; this is inferred, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No sourced sales-cycle lengths, enterprise ACVs, or CAC for any vendor; a16z/Bessemer/ICONIQ AI reports and Sifted were not retrieved; Accounting Today and Fintech Business Weekly not directly accessed.
